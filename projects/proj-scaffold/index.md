# {{page.title}} - {{page.description}}

[<tt>{{page.title}}</tt>] is tool to help students learn and master course material, and to help instructors structure 
course concepts and PrairieLearn assessments.

<details markdown="1">
<summary markdown="1">Click the triangle for more details about [<tt>{{page.title}}</tt>] capabilties</summary>

### [<tt>{{page.title}}</tt>] allows instructors to:

* Set up a graph of course concepts, subconcepts, and their pre-requisite relationships.
* Associate course concepts with PrairieLearn Assessments
* Upload a course roster of students and staff

Subsequently, enrolled students and authorized staff can: 
* Access the course concept graph
* Practice questions and learn concepts

This project was started by Kate Larrick as part of her MS project for her Master's degree in Computer Science at UCSB.  It is being
extended by Phill Conrad, and the students of CS156.
