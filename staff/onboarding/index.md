## Participation Requirement

CMPSC 156 is structured so that nearly all "lectures" and "discussion" are actually hands-on learning activities where students work in groups on software engineering activities.

As such, we typically need all staff (all TAs and ULAs) to be available in *every lecture* and *every discsusion section*.

If that will be an issue, please raise it with the instructor as soon as possible.

## Onboarding to systems

### Course Slack

The main way in which the staff communicate with one another and with the students is through a Slack Workspace.  

Look for an invitation to the course slack, and the `#staff` channel.  Please monitor the `#staff` channel and the `#all-ucsb-cs156-qxx` channel every univeristy work day throughout your appointment.  (Here, and throughout this document, `qxx` stands for the current quarter, e.g. `f26`).

(You are not required or expected to monitor the slack on weekends or university holidays, though you may choose to do so it if helps you to balance your workload.)

### Frontiers

We use a tool called `frontiers` (available at <https://frontiers.dokku-00.cs.ucsb.edu>) to manage the course and the Github organization for the course.

Please visit <https://frontiers.dokku-00.cs.ucsb.edu>, where you should see CS156 listed as a "staff" course.  Click the "Join Course" button and then follow the instructions.  This should issue you an invitation to the Github organization for the course with "owner" privileges.

### Canvas/Gradescope

You should receive an invitation to join the Canvas site for the course as TA.   

Once you accept this invitation, the instructor can "Sync Canvas Roster" so that you also have TA access in Gradescope.

## Roles of TAs and ULAs

TAs will typically lead the discussion sections.

The instructor will typically lead the lecture sections, while TAs will typically lead the discussion sections.  

The plan for each discussion section will typically be posted on the course website under "lectures", and/or disseminated in the course slack.
